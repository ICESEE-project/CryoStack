# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CryoStack is a research-software project. We want participation — filing
issues, proposing changes, asking questions, reviewing pull requests — to be
a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background, experience level, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in comments, issues, and reviews.
- Assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback focused on the work.
- Respect that this is a small, currently lightly staffed project — response
  times may be slow.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when representing the project in other public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, open a private report by
contacting a project maintainer directly through GitHub (a direct message to
a maintainer's GitHub account, since no public issue tracker is appropriate
for a conduct report). Reports will be reviewed and handled as
confidentially as the situation allows.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict a contributor's participation for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
