# About

CryoStack is a browser-based platform for cryosphere modeling, simulation, data assimilation, visualization, and scientific workflows.

The platform is being developed to make advanced cryosphere computing more accessible to researchers, students, and collaborators by connecting web-based interfaces to HPC and cloud resources.

## Platform goal

CryoStack provides a common entry point for launching and managing cryosphere applications.

Current workflows include:

- Ice-sheet model simulations
- ICESEE data assimilation workflows

Additional applications are planned as the platform grows.

## Collaborators

CryoStack is developed through the Ice, Climate, and Coasts Lab and the Polar Geophysics and Sea Level group.
